## 🎓 Application Guide

### Step 1 — Prep
Clean your natural lashes with an oil-free cleanser. 
Dry thoroughly before application.

### Step 2 — Measure & Trim
Measure lash clusters against your lash line. 
Trim from the inner edge if needed.

### Step 3 — Apply Adhesive
Apply a thin layer of lash glue to the cluster band. 
Wait 20–30 seconds until tacky.

### Step 4 — Placement
Using tweezers, place clusters just above your natural lash line. 
Press gently and hold for 5–10 seconds.

### Step 5 — Blend & Seal
Apply a coat of lash sealant over the bond line 
to extend wear up to 7 days.

## 💡 Pro Tips for Lash Artists

- **Storage**: Keep lashes in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight
- **Removal**: Always use an oil-based remover — never pull or tug
- **Layering**: Mix 2–3 curl types for a more natural, dimensional look
- **Client Aftercare**: No steam or oil-based products for 24 hours post-application
